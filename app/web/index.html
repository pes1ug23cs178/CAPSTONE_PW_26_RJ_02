How it works

1

We find the face in up to 32 moments of your video (or in your photo) and skip moments where the head is turned away.

2

Our model looks for 10 tell-tale signs of face swapping, such as a seam around the face or a mouth that looks edited.

3

We combine them into one answer and show you the strongest clues, with the place on the face where we found them.

No detector is perfect. GRACE learned from face-swap videos and can be fooled by new editing tools, heavy compression, or faces seen from the side. Use this as one piece of evidence, not the final word.

Privacy: your file is analysed on this server only for this check. It is deleted as soon as the check ends, and the result is removed after 30 minutes.
