## 1. Role and goal

## 4. The story (what the paper argues)

1. **Problem.** Detectors trained on FF++ score near 1.0 in-domain and drop sharply on unseen data, under
   compression, and they explain themselves with post-hoc heatmaps that are not guaranteed to be faithful.
2. **Protocol first.** All splits are by source identity with automated leakage tests; every selection decision uses
   FF++ validation only; test sets are touched once. *Case study:* our own Phase-2 checkpoint, reported at 97.3% validation accuracy, had
   trained on most of the FF++ val/test and DFD videos through a random video split (Table 6 in
   `results/tables.md`). Only its Celeb-DF column is a fair number.
3. **Method (GRACE).** Frozen CLIP ViT-L/14 + LoRA; a block-DCT frequency branch with gated fusion; a 10-concept
   bottleneck whose labels are the parameters of the self-blended-image (SBI) recipe that made each fake (blend
   type, mask shape, colour/blur/resampling, region), so concept labels cost nothing; a small residual path with an
   L2 penalty; boundary-map supervision. Final teacher F1 chosen on FF++ val.
4. **Distillation.** EfficientNet-B0 student trained on degraded frames with logit + concept + map distillation
   (S3), versus no teacher (S0) and logit-only KD (S2).
5. **Results** (Section 5 below) and **what did not work** (Section 6).
6. **Takeaways.** (i) The backbone change (Swin-B → CLIP+LoRA) gives most of the clean cross-dataset gain;
   (ii) the GRACE additions do not raise clean cross-dataset AUC over C1, but they keep it under compression
   (JPEG-30: 0.901 vs 0.777) and add concepts that point to the manipulated region; (iii) more diverse training
   data (X1 = G3 trained on FF++ + DFD) reaches 0.944 on Celeb-DF vs 0.856 for G3 and 0.878 for F1, more than any
   architecture change; (iv) domain-adversarial
   training hurt in every form we tried; (v) KD transfers concept behaviour but not accuracy.

## 6. Negative results (must be in the paper, compactly)

- SBI alone does not raise clean cross AUC (C1 0.914 → C2 0.909).
- FFT branch is worse than DCT (0.915 vs 0.930).
- DANN: on Swin-B with real as a domain 0.854 → 0.828; on the teacher's features G2-fft 0.915 → G3 0.907 (G3 was built on the FFT variant, chosen on val); on patch features
  F2 0.733. Unsupervised adaptation to Celeb-DF (D2) 0.878 → 0.832 on Celeb-DF.
- Full KD does not beat a teacher-free student on clean data (0.809 vs 0.804) — its benefit is concept agreement and
  a small JPEG gain.
- Deletion AUC of F1's concept map (0.984) is worse than random (0.908): the maps *localise* well but deleting the
  highlighted pixels does not lower the score. Say this plainly; do not call the maps "faithful" without qualification.
- Concept c10 is not learned (AUC ≈ 0.52); say so.
- H.264 CRF40 remains hard for every model (cross ≤ 0.644).

## 7. Claims: allowed vs forbidden

**Allowed:** "under our leak-free protocol"; "relative to its parent model"; "on the official Celeb-DF test list";
"seed mean ± std over 3 seeds" (only where 3 seeds exist).

**Forbidden:**
- Any "state-of-the-art" or "outperforms existing methods" claim. Published numbers use full FF++ (raw/c23/c40,
  five methods); ours use a reduced c23 subset. Put published numbers in a separate table labelled
  "reported numbers, different training data and protocol — not directly comparable".
- Calling a single-seed difference significant. Report bootstrap 95% CIs (1,000 resamples over videos) from
  `predictions_*.csv`, and a DeLong test for B1 vs F1 and C1 vs F1 on each test set.
- Saying explanations are "faithful" without the deletion caveat.
- Saying the student "matches" the teacher.
